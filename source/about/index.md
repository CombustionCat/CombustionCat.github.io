The answer to the never-ending questions on where to stargaze in Singapore.

I am unaffiliated with any astronomy organisation or school in Singapore, I just have a telescope and like walking around Singapore at night.

Feedback, questions or a site to add? Email [spacecatloaf@gmail.com](mailto:spacecatloaf@gmail.com). Will reply at earliest convenience (it is not very convenient).

## FAQ

### Why is it called Light Sky Sites?

It's a call to traditional Dark Sky Sites but we are in Singapore so we make do with what we have.

### Do the people running this site have any expertise to judge dark skies and low horizons?

About a decade of stargazing, comet hunting and setting up telescopes in Singapore.

### Why bother adding any places that are not very dark?

People are interested in stargazing with different priorities in Singapore. Some bright places are sufficient for the needs of others who prioritise other factors such as food, less insects, near MRT etc.
