## Sky Conditions

- **Low horizon:** east, south
- **Overall darkness:** 2/3
- **Dark regions:** south and east are relatively dark; north has street lights; compared to the Marine Parade pier, CBD is blocked — slightly darker overall

<img class="site-panorama" src="/images/ECPTanjongKatongPier/panorama.jpg" alt="ECP Tanjong Katong Pier panorama">

## Location Information

- **Food/drinks options:** food and drink nearby; check operating hours
- **Insects:** none (?)
- **Transportation:** 20–30 min walk from MRT; carpark nearby
- **Terrain accessibility:** paved ground; 20 mins from MRT, 5–10 mins from carpark
- **Regulations:** rope cordon (but there are other people fishing)
- **Shelter:** 10 min away
- **Time-gated:** no
- **Toilet:** yes (next to food)
- **Observation site:** paved

**Notes:** May need to coexist with fishermen. Darker than the Marine Parade pier.
