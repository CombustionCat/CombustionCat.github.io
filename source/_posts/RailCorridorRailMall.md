## Location Information

- **Food/drinks options:** Rail Mall
- **Insects:** many
- **Transportation:** 15 min from Hillview MRT; can park at Rail Mall but is an uphill walk — better for car owners
- **Terrain accessibility:** paved ground with gravel; steps from the carpark; lots of foot and bike traffic
- **Regulations:** none
- **Shelter:** sheltered walkway 10 min away

- **Toilet:** on the path
