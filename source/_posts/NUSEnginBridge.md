## Sky Conditions

- **Low horizon:** none
- **Overall darkness:** 1/3
- **Dark regions:** none; overhead is ok

## Location Information

- **Food/drinks options:** vending machine; 24h Cheers in NUS Engineering
- **Insects:** red ants and occasional mosquitos — do not go near the railing (red ants); bat droppings on floor
- **Transportation:** 1 min from bus stop; MRT (Kent Ridge or Clementi) is 5 bus stops away + NUS IT carpark or Engin carpark nearby, 5 min walk
- **Terrain accessibility:** paved ground with little gradient; 10 mins from dropoff point (NUS IT or Engin), need to go through lift
- **Regulations:** subject to NUS regulations
- **Shelter:** 1 min away
- **Time-gated:** no
- **Toilet:** yes
- **Observation site:** paved

**Notes:** Power available if you have a 6m power reel. The cable has to be dangled to the floor below.
