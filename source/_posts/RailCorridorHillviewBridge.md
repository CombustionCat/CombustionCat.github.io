## Sky Conditions

- **Low horizon:** none
- **Overall darkness:** 2/3
- **Dark regions:** north–south corridor is kind of dark; east to south-south-west is also available when walking up and down the corridor

<img class="site-panorama" src="/images/RailCorridorHillviewBridge/Paranoma.jpg" alt="Rail Corridor Hillview Bridge panorama">

## Location Information

- **Food/drinks options:** Hillv2, Rail Mall
- **Insects:** many
- **Transportation:** 5 min from Hillview MRT; can park at Hillv2 but is an uphill walk — public transport is easier
- **Terrain accessibility:** paved ground; slopes and ramps from MRT
- **Regulations:** none
- **Shelter:** sheltered walkway 5 min away
- **Time-gated:** no
- **Toilet:** MRT
- **Observation site:** paved

**Notes:** Site offers mobility in the north–south direction. Last updated 2025-07-18.
