## Location Information

- **Food/drinks options:** Golden Mile hawker centre across the road
- **Insects:** some — built-up area
- **Transportation:** 4 min from bus stop opposite St John HQ; 20 min walk from Bugis MRT + it is a carpark
- **Terrain accessibility:** paved ground, with lift; is a carpark
- **Regulations:** carpark — check carpark timing
- **Shelter:** 1 min away
- **Time-gated:** no
- **Toilet:** yes
- **Observation site:** paved

**Notes:** Walking directions — from street level, take the outside lift to the projector on level 5, walk towards the toilets/carpark, exit through the carpark door, turn right towards the skyline. Alternatively, from the level 5 carpark, look for the mural with "The Projector" written there; right in front of the mural turn left towards the city skyline.
